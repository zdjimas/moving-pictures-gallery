cjmurphy

Composition • Arrangement • Sound Design

Chris J. Murphy composes with a storyteller’s ear. His work here leans into texture—melody lines that carry memory, percussion that breathes, and arrangements that land like scenes from a life. The themes of recovery, resilience, and honest self-accounting that shape his writing also shape his audio: dynamic builds, quiet breaks, and payoffs that feel earned rather than forced.

As co-author and the voice behind Letters to My Dead Parents, Chris brings hard-won perspective to sound design—turning lived experience into cues, motifs, and atmospheres that pair with the visuals across Moving Pictures and KZAK. Expect hooks you can hum, space you can feel, and mixes that translate from headphones to car stereos to the gallery walls.
